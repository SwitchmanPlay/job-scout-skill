# applications.md template

Create it in "Job Scout" with whatever the person already applied to. The daily scout updates it from email; Claude updates it in chat when the person says they applied.

```markdown
# Applications
Last updated: {{DATE}}

## Applied
| Date | Employer | Role | Channel (portal, email, outreach, referral) | Email domain | Status | Last update |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Interviews and offers
| Date | Employer | Role | Stage | Next step or promised feedback date |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rejected or closed
| Date | Employer | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
```

"Email domain" is the domain the employer writes from (their own domain, or a recruiting system such as successfactors.eu or workday.com). The daily scout uses it to find replies, so fill it when known.
