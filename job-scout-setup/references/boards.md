# Job boards: examples to start from

These are examples, not a verified list. Sites change URLs and blocking often. The rule is: search for the right sources for this person, then test every one in Step 5. Quirks below were observed in autumn 2026 and may no longer hold. Entries marked "verify" are less certain.

## Works in most countries
- LinkedIn Jobs: biggest source in most fields; needs login and JavaScript. Cloud scout: alerts only.
- Indeed (country sites like de.indeed.com, uk.indeed.com, indeed.com): page 1 of a list often fetchable; click-through links (/rc/clk) are blocked by robots.txt. Set an alert.
- Glassdoor, Welcome to the Jungle, Wellfound (startups): mostly JavaScript; alerts.
- Employer career pages: Personio (<org>.jobs.personio.com or .de), Greenhouse (job-boards.greenhouse.io/<org> or the .eu variant), Lever (jobs.lever.co/<org> or jobs.eu.lever.co) are usually fetchable; Workday and SuccessFactors usually are not.
- The country's public-sector job portal and the national employment agency.
- The professional association of the field (most run a job board).
- University career centres and student union boards, for students and graduates.

## Austria (examples)
- karriere.at: fetchable; add ?sort=date. One fetch sees only about the first 15 results and paging did not work by fetch, so use several narrow searches. Pattern /jobs/<keyword>/<city>.
- StepStone.at: page 1 fetchable; robots.txt forbids deeper pages.
- jobs.at, absolventen.at, unijobs.at: list pages fetchable.
- AMS job portal (jobs.ams.at, verify): JavaScript; browser or skip.
- willhaben Jobs: alerts.
- Public sector: Karriere beim Bund (jobboerse.gv.at, verify), City of Vienna (jobs.wien.gv.at), Austrian Parliament careers page.

## Germany (examples)
- StepStone.de, Indeed.de, Bundesagentur für Arbeit Jobsuche (JavaScript), XING (login), Stellenwerk (student jobs).
- Public sector: interamt.de, service.bund.de.

## United Kingdom (examples)
- Indeed UK, Reed, Totaljobs, Guardian Jobs, CharityJob, Civil Service Jobs, W4MP (jobs with MPs and in politics), jobs.ac.uk.

## United States (examples)
- Indeed, LinkedIn, USAJOBS (federal), GovernmentJobs (state and local), Idealist (nonprofits), Handshake (students, needs university login), Tom Manatos Jobs and Roll Call jobs (Capitol Hill, verify).

## Policy, politics, NGOs, international organisations
- EU: EU Careers / EPSO, EuroBrussels, Euractiv Jobs. Traineeships with fixed annual windows: Commission Blue Book, European Parliament Schuman traineeships. Set deadline reminders for these.
- UN and international organisations: UN Careers, Impactpool, Devex, ReliefWeb (humanitarian), OSCE, OECD, Council of Europe career pages.
- NGOs and foundations: Idealist, CharityJob, national NGO umbrella organisations.
- Parliaments, ministries, party offices and think tanks mostly post on their own sites; collect the relevant ones as Tier B. Many accept speculative applications for assistant roles, which suits the outreach part.

## Tech
- WeAreDevelopers, devjobs.at / germantechjobs.de / swissdevjobs.ch, startup.jobs, Otta (Welcome to the Jungle).

## Research and academia
- Euraxess, jobs.ac.uk, academicpositions.com, HigherEdJobs (US), university and institute job pages.

## Any other field or country
Search "<role> jobs <city>", "<field> job board <country>", the national professional association, the field's trade press, and the 10 to 20 employers that matter most. Ask the person where they found their last job or where colleagues look.
